# VA Analytics Contract: Amendment 3

**Amendment 3, November 2026. What is kept.**

1. Analytics stay at session level, as the Analytics Scope says: one session record for each conversation, with no identifiers, kept in the hosting's logs for as long as it keeps them (Data Minimisation and Lawful Basis Note v2.0, section 9). The answers added by that note are kept only as counts, not in the session record.
2. A count of ethnicity by council area and census group is permitted, for reach and equity, with the purpose stated to the person when asked (Data Minimisation and Lawful Basis Note v2.0, section 6). Counts that could point to an individual are left out of reports.

To be folded into the consolidated contract at its next version. Authority: Managing Director, 8 November 2026, with the Data Minimisation and Lawful Basis Note v2.0.
